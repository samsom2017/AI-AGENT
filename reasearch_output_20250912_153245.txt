ERITREA POPULATION RESEARCH

Topic: Population of Eritrea

Key Findings:
- There is significant disagreement among sources regarding Eritrea's current population
- Population estimates range from 3.5 million to 6.7 million people
- Some sources cite estimates as low as 3.6 million and as high as 6.4-6.7 million
- Eritrea has never conducted an official government census
- The last census conducted was the 1984 Ethiopian census (before Eritrea's independence in 1993)

Demographic Information:
- Eritrea has nine recognized ethnic groups
- The Tigrinya are the largest ethnic group, constituting about 50% of the population
- The majority of Tigrinya people inhabit the highlands of Eritrea
- Migration within the country has occurred over time

Refugee Statistics:
- As of May 2024, over 170,000 Eritreans were registered as refugees in Ethiopia
- Eritrean refugees have faced challenges and insecurity in camps, particularly in Ethiopia's conflict-affected Amhara region

Note: Due to the lack of official census data, population figures for Eritrea remain estimates and should be considered approximate.
